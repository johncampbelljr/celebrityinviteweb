🪩 How it works

Three steps to the A-list

One phone between you. One star at a time. Every decision defended out loud.

1 🌟

Deal a star

One player is the Dealer: they hold the phone the whole game, dealing one celebrity at a time — movie stars, music icons, athletes, world leaders — and tapping in every verdict.

2 🗣️

Rule — and say why

The other player is the Host — and they never touch the phone. They rule each star out loud, in or out, with a reason attached. "He'd bring the espresso" counts. Silence doesn't.

3 🥳

Party, then swap

The tenth yes drops the confetti and reveals the Host's Party Page. Next round, trade roles. Playing with a crowd? The whole room debates every seat.
